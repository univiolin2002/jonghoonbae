Jong Hoon Bae · Conductor

Oxford

On 6 February 2027 Jong Hoon Bae and the Seocho Philharmonia appear at the invitation of the University of Oxford with a programme of Haydn symphonies at the Sohmen Concert Hall, the 500-seat hall of the Stephen A. Schwarzman Centre for the Humanities, opened in 2025.

Seoul · 2027

Following the completion of the Haydn cycle, the Seocho Philharmonia begins two further traversals in 2027: from March, a monthly cycle of the complete Beethoven symphonies, each paired with a Beethoven concerto; and the first season of a three-year cycle of the Mahler symphonies.

Haydn · 2032

A recorded cycle of the complete Haydn symphonies, prepared with historically informed performance scholars at Oxford, is in preparation for release ahead of the tercentenary of Haydn’s birth in 2032.

Season 2027

  • 6 FebHaydn symphonies — Sohmen Concert Hall, University of Oxford
  • FebNew Year Concert — Seocho Philharmonia, Seoul
  • Mar–The complete Beethoven symphonies, monthly, with the concertos — Seocho Philharmonia, Seoul
  • 2027–29The complete Mahler symphonies — Seocho Philharmonia, Seoul

Dates and programmes are announced by the Seocho Cultural Foundation and may change.
